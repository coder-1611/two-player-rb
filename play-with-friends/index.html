RETRO BOWL 2P

PLAY RETRO BOWL WITH FRIENDS

Retro Bowl was built as a one-player game. Retro Bowl 2P lets two people play each other from separate devices, anywhere, in the browser. You run your offense on your screen. Your friend runs theirs on their screen. The score, the clock and the ball position stay in sync between the two.

CREATE A ROOM →

THE ROOM CODE

Every game lives in a room with a 4-letter code. The person who taps PLAY 2P gets the code on their screen. Send it any way you like: say it out loud, text it, paste it in a chat. The second player types it in and taps JOIN. Rooms are private to whoever has the code.

WHO PLAYS WHAT

  • Player A starts with San Francisco, Player B with Pittsburgh. Rosters are real NFL-style lineups with names, positions and ratings.
  • Each drive is played by the person who has the ball. The other player watches the live score and waits for the hand-off. Nothing about the opponent's drive is simulated.
  • After a touchdown, the scorer plays their own extra point or two-point try.
  • Same room, another game: when a match ends you can run it back without making a new room.

WHAT YOU NEED

  • Any device with a browser: Chromebook, laptop, iPhone, Android.
  • An internet connection on both ends. School and home Wi-Fi both work.
  • About one minute to set up. No account, no download, no payment.

DIFFICULTY

Before you create a room you choose how the defense plays against both of you: SAME gives both players identical defense difficulty, DIFFERENT lets each player pick their own. Handy when one of you has played a lot more Retro Bowl than the other.

Friend not online right now? Play the single-player game and come back with a room code later.
