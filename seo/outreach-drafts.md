## r/RetroBowl (Reddit)

**Title:** I made a way to play Retro Bowl against a friend (two phones, one game)

**Body:**
Retro Bowl is my favorite game but it's single-player only, so I built a two-player version:
one person taps PLAY 2P and sends the invite link, the other opens it, and you play each other.
You're on offense on your phone while your friend plays defense on theirs, then it swaps.
Pick-sixes, 2-point conversions, overtime and the full stats screen at the end all work.

It's free and runs in the browser: https://two-player-rb.vercel.app

It's a fan project; there's a bug-report button (the "!" in the corner) if anything breaks.
Would love to hear what you think.
