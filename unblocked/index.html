This is Retro Bowl you can play against a real person, straight from a browser tab. Nothing to install, no account, no app store. Open the page, tap PLAY 2P, send your friend the 4-letter room code, and you are both on the field within a minute.

Free. Works on Chromebook, Windows, Mac, iPhone and Android.

WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT

  • Two humans, two devices. Every drive is played by a person. There is no computer running the other team's offense. When it is your friend's turn with the ball, your screen waits and shows the live scoreboard.
  • Room codes, not accounts. One player creates a room and gets a code like KAJU. The other types it in. That is the entire setup.
  • Runs in a normal tab. It is a single web page. A school or library Chromebook that can open a website can open this.
  • Defense difficulty for both players. Pick the same difficulty for both sides, or let each player choose their own.

HOW TO START A 2 PLAYER GAME

  1. Open two-player-rb.vercel.app on both devices.
  2. Player one taps PLAY 2P and reads out the 4-letter room code.
  3. Player two types the code under HAVE A CODE? JOIN A FRIEND'S ROOM and taps JOIN.
  4. Both players enter a name (it goes next to your team on the game record), then kick off.
Nobody around to play? The plain single-player game is at main-retro-bowl.vercel.app.

FAQ

Does it work on a Chromebook? Yes. Most of the games played here are on Chromebooks. Drag to throw with the trackpad or a mouse.

Do I need to sign up? No. You only type a display name so your friend can see who they are playing.

Can we play on phones? Yes. The page rotates to landscape on a phone and plays with touch.

Is this the official Retro Bowl? No. It is a fan-made two-player mode built on the browser version of the game. The official game by New Star Games is on the App Store, Google Play and Poki.

Something broke mid-game. Tap the ! button in the bottom-left corner. Your report arrives with the game's own log attached, so it can actually be fixed.
