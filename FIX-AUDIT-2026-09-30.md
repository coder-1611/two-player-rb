## Data and method

- **Archive:** a snapshot of `audits/*.json` copied at 2026-09-30 15:11 CDT (1,036 rooms). The live archive kept growing while I worked, so every number below comes from the frozen copy in `scratchpad/audits-snap/`.

  No remaining room had only harness binds (`src:'local'`, localhost, HeadlessChrome, `test`).

  - The checker's cut also splits a room at a reload's `TURN-> a (match-start)` line, so a few "games" are reloads, not new games (KWYL g2/g3, VVLQ g3, EQXQ g2).
- **Build of a game:** the newest `ver` among the binds in effect. That is the binds inside the segment plus each role's previous bind, when that role's old page wrote entries in the segment. 21 games are mixed builds (a partner on a cached older page, e.g. WNPB g2 V422/V424, HEIG V422/V424).

- **Verdicts:**
  - Firings were found by exact log strings. They were taken from `git diff` of `index.html` between the ship commits and cross-checked against the ledger.
  - Every firing's ±30 s window was scored automatically: the next snap or stats screen, any counted R-FREEZE interval starting within 30 s, R-SCORE/R-GIFT/R-HALF flags, and the players leaving.

## Findings confirmed and counted

### (a) The rematch "FLOW healed" kills the new game

**6 games** had `FLOW healed my last send` within 30 s of a `game` entry. EQXQ g2 (V423) was a reload after the game had already ended, with nothing to play. The other **5 are real V424 rematches, and all 5 froze:**

|---|---|---|---|
| NPJD g2 (09-30 10:01 CDT) | 0.5 s, both phones | B "hand-off not applied"; A parked at 0:14 after its first play; lost-send 0:17.8; both left 0:18–0:29 | 13 s, permanent |
| ZJCF g2 (09-30 13:20) | 0.3 s, both phones | A parked 0:07; lost-send 0:18; "both parked, no decision" 33 s; left at 0:58 | 46 s, permanent |
| WNPB g2 (09-30 09:37, A V424 / B V422) | 0.5 s, both phones | A parked 0:06; lost-send 0:16; TURN-RESCUE deferred ×10; B left at 1:25 | 53 s, permanent |

| JKYT g4 (09-30 11:07) | 0.2 s | B "hand-off not applied" 11 s; lost-send 0:15.7 cleared it; A was mid-play and never parked | 11 s, temporary; the game completed (62 snaps) |

- **The chain in each case:** at GAME-START each page restores its previous-game flow record (`FLOW restored from this tab pending ep K0 seq N`). The partner's `staged` value from the old game "heals" a phantom `HEALED` send (ts from the old game). The partner's authority must apply it, but it is not on the server. The live opening-kickoff phone is parked, the 3-look lost-send leaves "no decision", and the old rescuers defer.
- **When it happens:** 4 of the 5 followed a previous game that ended with no stats screen (abandoned mid-game): all 4 such V424 rematches healed. After a completed game it happened 1 time in 19. The one V423 rematch after an abandoned game (DAXK g5) did not heal.
- **Rates:** V424 rematches 5/23 healed at start (all frozen); V421–V423 rematches 0/19 (EQXQ aside).
- **Totals:** the heal's knock-on accounts for 5 of V424's 7 frozen games and 8 of its 12 counted intervals.

### (b) The overtime try credited as +6 (a loop)

The pattern: `OT-TD touchdown in overtime (x -> x+6)`, then a try (`CONV try started`, snap at down 6 from the 2), then **the scorer's own score +6 again**. The detector reads that +6 as a new touchdown and offers another 1 PT / 2 PT, so a player can loop.

| game | build | scorer | sequence | repeated +6 | complete |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| EXYT g1 | V424 | a | 46→52 (TD) →58 | 1 | yes (58–52; A wins either way) |
| FTYF g1 | V424 | a | 14→20 (TD) →26→32→38 | 3 | no |
| KHIX g3 | V424 | a | 38→44 (TD) →50 | 1 | no |
| VVLQ g2 | V424 | a | 38→44 (TD) →50→56→62 | 3 | no; the reload into this loop became VVLQ g3's permanent freeze |
| JYRF g1 | V421 | a | 44→50 (TD) →56→62→68 | 3 | no |
| KTYA g1 | V415 | b | 30→36 | 1 | yes (36–30; B wins either way) |
| ZUKY g1 | V416 | a | 32→38 | 1 | no |

- **On V424:** 4 games, 8 repeated +6 = **48 points credited to tries.** A try is worth at most 2, and only one per touchdown, so **at least 40 extra points** (8 legitimate at most).
- **Before V424:** 3 games, 5 repeats, 30 points, at least 24 extra.
- **The V424 engine patch did not change it:** the +6 appears on V424 at the same rate as before (4 of 6 V424 games with an OT touchdown; 3 of 6 before). The real-game credit goes to the scorer, not the "other team" the V424 probe described.
- **No completed game changed winner** (only EXYT and KTYA were completed). The 5 others were abandoned.
- **What V424 did fix:** the "try never starts" part. 9/9 V424 overtime touchdowns got a try.
